Latep — ZK Prisoner's Dilemma on Stellar

Hackathon submission for "Stellar Hacks: Real-World ZK". A 2:30 walkthrough of a working app that uses two zero-knowledge primitives, both verified on-chain by a Soroban contract on Stellar testnet.

KEY MESSAGE
Trust is proven, not promised. Two ZK primitives make the app real:
1. Private accreditation — prove you're on the allowlist without revealing which credential is yours. Poseidon Merkle tree + nullifier, verified on-chain via UltraHonk. This maps directly to the "identity / compliance proofs" example the hackathon called out.
2. Binding move commitment — a ZK proof makes the commit binding at commit time. Rejects garbage commitments immediately, before the opponent locks their stake. UltraHonk proof of keccak256(move || nonce || game_id) with move ∈ {0, 1}.
